The draft installs a worker-owned SIGTERM handler that records a request instead
of raising asynchronously during subprocess construction. The worker launches its
selector in a new session, checks cancellation while collecting anonymous-pipe
output, stops the owned process group on cancellation, and waits for the selector
and adopted descendants. Cleanup failure retains the triggering exception as its
cause. It does not publish partial selector output as a normal result.

Scope limitations: process-group shutdown does not cover descendants deliberately
leaving that group. Such descendants must cause non-quiescence, not a PASS.
This primitive assumes a dedicated worker with subreaper ownership; it must not be
installed in the general controller or a process with unrelated children.
Mounted /proc PIDs are never used for signalling.
