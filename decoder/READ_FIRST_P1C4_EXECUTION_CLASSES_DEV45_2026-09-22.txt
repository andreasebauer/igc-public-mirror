1. FOUR EXECUTION CLASSES

FRESH_RECOMPUTE
  Registered code executes the declared target again from permitted inputs.
  This is the only class that may count toward empty-root science replay.

VERIFIED_RESTORED_BLOCK
  A previously produced block is restored or embedded and verified. Its
  science is not regenerated from an empty generated-data root.

SOURCE_INTEGRITY_ONLY
  Pinned theorem, design or documentary bytes and declarations are checked.

HISTORICAL_RESULT_ONLY
  A frozen historical result or disposition is compared/preserved without
  regenerating its science.
