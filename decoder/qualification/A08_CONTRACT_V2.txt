A08: EXPLICIT_ARTIFACT_CONTENT_V1, dev133 candidate

New SCIENCE admission requires IG_DECODER_RESULT_CONTRACT_V2 and a nonempty,
unique scientific_content list naming required_artifacts. Every named artifact
must have an expected sha256 or a value/shape check beyond the top-level process
status/return_code/exit_code. Top-level outcome assertions must constrain
non-process outcome strings. Process-result checks alone cannot qualify content.
All declared checks remain independently mandatory, including supporting-file
checks. Unlisted artifacts are SUPPORTING, never silently content-certified.

The policy is a syntactic admission floor. Arbitrary assertions and frozen hashes
establish declared-content conformance only. It cannot judge theorem truth,
representativeness or whether a caller has mislabeled process data as science.
V2 verification explicitly grants science_qualification_authority NONE.
NO_EFFECT and NOT_ESTABLISHED remain valid registered outcomes. SCRIPT process outcomes remain registered alongside scientific outcomes, as
required by controller admission. At least one non-process outcome is required;
process outcomes cannot satisfy scientific verification or be reported as a
scientific conclusion. This repairs the dev132 565 PASS / 1 FAIL attempt, retained
unchanged with its rejected completion.

New admission of every V1 SCIENCE contract refuses, including historically strong
contracts. Migration requires a new explicit V2 contract and new capture; no
automatic inference, alteration or rerun of old captures. Existing V1 VALIDATION
and EXECUTION_ONLY contract/report semantics remain unchanged. Existing immutable
completion verification reads original evidence/hashes without re-evaluating the
science contract under new semantics. Historical source and receipts are retained.
Unfinished legacy SCIENCE work requires explicit migration to resume on dev133.

normalize, stored contract_for and direct verify enforce the same V2 content
policy. Stored question outcomes must equal the normalized declaration.
Science JSON decoded for checks or outcome extraction is UTF-8, rejects duplicate
keys at any depth, NaN/Infinity and overflow to infinity. Hash-only opaque artifacts
are byte-constrained, not claimed to be parsed JSON. Supporting JSON is strict when
it is decoded. General historical parsers and A06 full scope are not closed here.
