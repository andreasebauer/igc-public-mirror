3.1 Fixed-field closure is theorem-derived
------------------------------------------
For any fixed value of L, s, and stay, the frozen output rules use idempotent operations:

    L_out    = L_A OR L_B
    s_out    = min(s_A,s_B)
    stay_out = stay_A AND stay_B

Therefore, once both inputs have exactly L=15,s=1,stay=0:

    15 OR 15 = 15
    min(1,1) = 1

This is a direct corollary of the already-established algebra. It does not by itself make the number 15 special and is not the principal mature-node discovery.
