2. THE SCIENTIFIC QUESTION

Can a retained relation BETWEEN complete G7 units carry information that their
separate exact G7 descriptions do not carry, and can that information affect
complete future observations under unchanged local G7 attachments?

There are two deliberately separate questions:
(a) Does a proposed joint representation contain more than the marginals?
(b) Does the COMMON inherited action/flat observer actually detect a difference?
A positive answer to (a) by construction does not answer (b).

unique next layer dictated by the original algebra.

3. FROZEN G7 PARENT

A parent presentation is (T,R). T is a complete feasible, marker-free,
C-labelled typed tree in the inherited four-seed generated domain. R is a
nonempty finite SET of admissible edge-cut rows. Each cut row partitions T
into connected generated blocks. The empty cut ROW is valid and means one
block; the empty FAMILY is invalid. One typed-tree isomorphism transports
all rows together. Local resource capacities and the original 31-operator
basis remain those of the source; no capacities are invented or replenished.

For a cut row C define m_T(C) as the minimum block SIZE and E_T(C) as the
union of owners in blocks attaining that minimum. This is not the number of
live owners. A local G7 action supplies a generated tree K as ONE new block
and an admitted endpoint-type operator (a,b). Owners v in T and w in K are
internal alternatives, NOT additional public action labels.

For each resource-legal pair (v,w), retain R_v={C in R: v in E_T(C)}.
If R_v is empty there is no successor for that role. Otherwise graft disjoint
copies at (v,w), retain the old cuts, and add the new bridge e as a cut in every
surviving row. One role yields ONE whole-family child. Deduplicate equal whole
children only. Do not split rows into successor branches, pool different child
states, reset to all decompositions, merge old blocks, or normalize by N.
The parent immediate observer is the exact flat class [T].

5. BRANCH A — INDEPENDENT_G7_COLLECTION_V1

  Omega=product_i R_i.
Formation: embed a supplied G7 state as a singleton component; combine disjoint
collections by Cartesian product of their relations. The result retains both

Written product-invariance deduction. For a fixed local role on component i,
filtering a Cartesian product by a predicate of C_i alone restricts only its

ith family times the untouched factors. An empty factor gives no successor.

rectangularity in every whole successor. Induction gives the same statement
for any finite construction in this grammar. This is an elementary written
deduction from the definitions, NOT a new executed or Lean-checked theorem.
Consequently that grammar does not spontaneously create cross-unit correlation.
