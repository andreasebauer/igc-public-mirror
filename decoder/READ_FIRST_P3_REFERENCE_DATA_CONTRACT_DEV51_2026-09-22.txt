1. RESULT

P3 defines one strict, content-addressed reference-data contract for all eleven
required replay categories: canonical objects, mechanisms, graduations, S/R/C/F
evidence, negative results, audit authorizations, earned-algebra statements,
dependency links, generation recipes, old/new comparisons and resume frontiers.

Every record has a stable namespaced ID, canonical SHA-256 identity, explicit
scope and nonclaims, exact provenance classification, dependencies, epistemic
status, science-execution status and authority effect. Type-specific payloads
are closed schemas; unknown or missing fields fail closed.

2. EMPTY ROOT AND PROVENANCE

The frozen empty root has no records, no invented carrier or correlation seed,
and science_executed=false. Its canonical manifest identity is:

  6c30e064452567707dc410a214c3fad3bd219231f5ebf998a9651c8f9a0ad91b

Provenance distinguishes derived theorem, certified construction, finite
computational observation, imported assumption, external audit decision and
unexplained. Unexplained records must remain OPEN. Audit authorization records
require external-audit provenance; ordinary Decoder records cannot record an
external decision.

3. EQUALITY, NEGATIVES AND SCIENCE STATE

Equality records explicitly bind set, multiset, count, byte, canonical-JSON or
certified-semantic meaning, cardinality semantics, compression and observer.
Negative results keep their tested scope and required nonclaims; they cannot be
relabelled as graduations. Reproduction is not represented as confirmation.

Each record says whether scientific execution occurred. The manifest's
science_executed bit is derived from its indexed records and rechecked during a
cold restore; it cannot be asserted independently.

4. DURABILITY

Records are immutable and content addressed. Dependencies must already exist in
manifest order. Writes use a transaction, immutable record, manifest and commit
sequence. An interrupted write is verified and recovered exactly once. A cold
restore rehashes every indexed record and rederives type counts, dependency
order and science-execution state. Unindexed content-store bytes are not
authoritative.
