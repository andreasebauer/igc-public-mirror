1. WHAT CHANGED

The P1A2 S/R/C/F catalogue compiler now accepts V3 historical assertion
mappings. Every mapping retains its own canonical obligation binding,
historical aliases, exact source hashes, locator, claim summary,
classification reason, scope/bounds, expected outcome, evidence mode and
mapping status.

Mapped, ambiguous and excluded records are distinct. Ambiguities block the
compiled replay DAG. Excluded records cannot bind a scientific obligation and
must use a category already declared in excluded_from_scientific_catalogue.
Bindings are reciprocal: a scientific node and every assertion mapped to it
must name one another.

Local-source qualification verifies the bytes of every pinned source. This is
an integrity check only; it does not interpret historical science and does not
create an audit authorization.
