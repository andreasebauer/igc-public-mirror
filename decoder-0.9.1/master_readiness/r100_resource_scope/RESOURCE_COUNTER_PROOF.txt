RESOURCE COUNTER FACTORISATION — EXPLICIT TREE SCHEMA
Human structural induction argument, with separate finite native conformance.
This is not a machine-checked universal native implementation certificate.

Domain. A leaf is SITE(p,f), with a seven-component nonnegative integral free
capacity vector. An internal resource is a tag and a finite unordered multiset
of child OCCURRENCES. Tags preserve the O2/O3/O4/O5/R6/R7/... hierarchy.
One admitted reservation of type t chooses one leaf occurrence with f[t]>0 and
subtracts one from that coordinate. It counts once even when f[t]>1. Hidden
edge incidence, source provenance, scanner construction witnesses and topology
are not part of this declared resource-action domain.

Reservation lemma. Any isomorphism of tagged unordered resource trees maps
leaf occurrences bijectively, preserving p, f and the chosen endpoint type.
It therefore maps admitted choices bijectively. Decrementing matched leaves
produces isomorphic successor trees. Grouped successor Counters are equal.
This holds at a leaf by direct equality of (p,f). For an internal node, its
isomorphism is a permutation of child occurrences plus a child isomorphism for
each matched pair. The admissible leaf-choice set is the disjoint union of
those child choice sets. Apply the induction hypothesis to each child and sum,
retaining the outer occurrence even when two children are equal. The reserve
function implements exactly this finite recursion, accumulating collisions.

Pair lemma. A current-level relation action chooses distinct owner occurrences
i<j and a Bridge assignment (a,b), then independently chooses one admitted
reservation in each selected owner. Its observable label is
(parent_tag,min(a,b),max(a,b)). Its projected successor replaces those two child
resources and retains every other occurrence. Under a child permutation, map
the unordered owner pair to its image. If its canonical index order reverses,
replace (a,b) with (b,a). Reversal closure of Bridge is required. The label is
unchanged, the choice-pair mapping is bijective, and the resulting unordered
child multiset is equal. Thus the complete labelled successor-tree Counters
agree on each lossless-resource-tree fibre. Distinct choices projecting to the
same key add their weights. Weighted orbit aggregation follows only when each
orbit representative preserves the projected successor and its weight is its
number of site occurrences. Native orbit/fast-kernel correctness is a separate
implementation-refinement obligation, not supplied by this abstract proof.

Hierarchy. Structural induction applies the reservation lemma inside every
child occurrence of a higher lift. The pair lemma then applies at that level.
No level-specific topology theorem is needed for this declared action domain.
The tree counter functions are constructive specifications of these recurrences.

Hash boundary. The native leaf codec hashes SITE|compact_JSON([p,f]). Internal
nodes hash tag|sorted_child_hashes, including duplicates. Equal lossless trees
have equal native digests without any collision assumption. Equal digests imply
equal lossless trees only under an injectivity premise. The native qualification
checks this on the finite saved R100 parent resource closure by retaining every
exact node payload by digest and rejecting conflicting payloads. This proves no
observed codec collision in that closure. It is not a global SHA-256 injectivity
theorem. Equality of successor Counter pushforwards follows from tree Counter
equality even when distinct successor trees hash together: contributions sum.

Native boundary. The independent materialized reference and fast kernel are
checked on captured finite native sentinels. The scanner-to-exact mapping also
preserves saved projections, capacities and construction provenance. Neither
test alone is universal refinement of every native action, action legality,
pointed-orbit grouping, incidence write, or read contract. No claim that a
resource-only profile validates all hidden topology writes is made.

V2 exclusion. RESOURCE_SCANNER_RELATION_ADD_AGGREGATE_V2 explicitly reads top
pairs and typed edges. Equal resource trees do not imply equal topologies.
Path/star carriers with identical child resources are a proposed native witness:
their resource successor Counter agrees, while V2 service diagnostics differ.
If verified, no resource-only factorisation law can authorize that V2 transport.
One must retain the topology/service read scope or remove theorem transport
consumption from a revised, separately qualified direct-observation route.
No old certificate is rebound, no baseline is reset and no production authority
is granted by this argument.
