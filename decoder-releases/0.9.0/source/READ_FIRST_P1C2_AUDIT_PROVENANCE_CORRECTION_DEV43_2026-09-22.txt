1. CORRECTION

Dev41 treated one broad user replay decision as the audit provenance for all
15 pre-GLOBAL layers. Dev43 no longer describes that as fifteen recorded layer
audits. It separates four facts:

- who authorized replay;
- whether a layer-specific audit record is actually pinned;
- whether the pinned record covers the whole combined layer;
- whether an independent replay is recorded.

The user decision still authorizes replay through the qualified G8 boundary.
It is now labelled USER_BLANKET_REPLAY_AUTHORIZATION wherever that is the
actual authority basis.

Every P1C2 authorization carries its authority basis, audit-record status,
audit-evidence pins, complete-layer flag and independent-replay flag. The
compiler validates those fields and local verification fails closed on a
missing or altered audit record.
